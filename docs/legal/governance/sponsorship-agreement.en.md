### Article 1 — Subject

The sponsor undertakes to pay the association the sponsorship sum, or to provide the goods or services in Article 4, and the association undertakes to provide the sponsor with the promotional consideration in Annex 1 (the package), in connection with [name and date of the event / the association's programme for the year / the campaign], in accordance with this agreement.

Sponsorship is a contract with consideration and is not a donation. The sponsorship sum goes to the association's Operations Fund and is used for organising events and running the association; it does not go to the Impact Fund and is not paid out to beneficiaries. The association publishes the sponsorship in the public ledger with the sponsor's name, the amount or value and the purpose.

### Article 2 — Package and consideration

The association's consideration is set out in Annex 1 by package tier [Core Cost Partner / Title Partner / Gold / Silver / Local Business / Match Partner / In kind]. Every package includes at least: a report to the sponsor within 30 days of the event (photographs with the sponsor's people, the number of participants, media coverage, the funds raised and where they went); a slot for a team of the sponsor's employees on the association's platform; an invitation to the public handover of funds to beneficiaries.

The association provides the consideration in the agreed form, at the agreed places and in the agreed period, with due care, and records the delivery of every item. If it cannot provide an item (for example because the event is cancelled), Article 9 applies.

### Article 3 — What sponsorship does not give

Sponsorship does not give the sponsor: influence over who receives the association's funds, which the Grants Committee alone decides under the published criteria; influence over the choice of beneficiaries, staff, members of bodies or suppliers; access to data on donors, participants, beneficiaries or volunteers; the right to have the association promote products or messages contrary to the association's goals, the Code of Conduct or the Child Safeguarding Policy; exclusivity, unless expressly agreed in Annex 1; the right to use the association's name, mark or materials beyond Article 6.

The association reserves the right to refuse or stop displaying sponsor material that is unlawful, offensive, aimed at children in an inappropriate way, or contrary to Article 12.

### Article 6 — Use of names and marks

The association has the right to use the sponsor's name and mark for the consideration in Annex 1, in the form the sponsor supplies, unaltered, for the term of the agreement and in reports and archives afterwards. The sponsor has the right, for the term of the agreement, to state that it is a sponsor of the association or the event and to use the association's mark and the designation in Annex 2 (for example “Official partner of Santamore Santa Run 2026”), in the form and under the rules in Annex 2, unaltered and without giving the impression that the association endorses the sponsor's products. Any other use of the association's name, mark, photographs or materials requires prior written approval. On termination of the agreement the sponsor ceases any new use, and material already published may remain.

Photographs from the event that the association supplies to the sponsor for the report and for posts are used by the sponsor only with credit to the association, only for non-commercial communication about the sponsorship, and only where the people shown have given consent; the sponsor does not publish photographs of children without the association's specific written approval.

### Article 7 — Term

The agreement enters into force on signature and runs [until all obligations in connection with the event are performed, and at the latest until the date / until 31 December of the year for an annual partnership]. The parties will discuss renewal no later than [[PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 60]] days before expiry; renewal is not automatic.

### Article 8 — Sponsor's duties

The sponsor: pays the sponsorship sum and delivers the goods or services on time; supplies the logo, texts and materials in the required format no later than [[PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 21]] days before the event, because materials supplied later may not make it to print; names a contact person; observes the Code of Conduct and the Child Safeguarding Policy for its representatives and promoters at the event, including the rule that promoters do not approach children without their parents; does not collect participants' data at the event without the participants' consent and without the association's approval; does not run activities at the event beyond those agreed; informs the association of any change that may affect the agreement or the reputation of the event.

### Article 9 — Cancellation, postponement and changes to the event

If the association postpones the event, the consideration is provided on the new date; if it cancels the event for reasons beyond its control (weather, a decision of a competent authority, force majeure), the association provides the consideration that is still possible (posts, the report, the team on the platform) and agrees with the sponsor: carrying the sponsorship over to the next event, a proportional reduction for the items not provided, or conversion into a donation to the Impact Fund, at the sponsor's choice. If the association cancels the event for reasons within its control, it refunds the sponsor the proportional part of the sum for the items not provided within 30 days. If the sponsor withdraws after signature, it pays the costs the association has already incurred in connection with its package (printing, production), up to a maximum of [[PLACEHOLDER: percentage]] percent of the sum.

### Article 10 — Liability

Each party is liable to the other for damage it causes by breach of the agreement, up to the amount of the sponsorship sum, except for damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence. The association is not liable for the sponsor's business results or for the extent of media coverage. The sponsor is liable for the goods and services it delivers and for the conduct of its people at the event. Nothing in this Article excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

### Article 11 — Confidentiality and data

The terms of this agreement are confidential, except for the sponsor's name, the package tier and the amount or value, which the association publishes in the public ledger and the annual report in accordance with its transparency policy, to which the sponsor consents. The association does not give the sponsor personal data of donors, participants, beneficiaries or volunteers; the team of the sponsor's employees on the platform sees only what all team members see. If the sponsor collects personal data in connection with the event (a prize draw, sign-ups), it is the controller of that data and informs the persons concerned accordingly.

### Article 12 — Ethics and termination

The association may terminate the agreement without notice, keeping the part already paid for the items provided and refunding the rest, if: the sponsor or its representatives breach Article 3, Article 8 or the Code of Conduct; proceedings are brought against the sponsor or a circumstance is made public that would make continued cooperation harmful to the association's reputation; the sponsor attempts to influence the choice of beneficiaries or the decisions of the association's bodies; the sponsor fails to pay the sum even within an additional period of 15 days. The sponsor may terminate the agreement without notice if the association materially breaches Article 2 and does not remedy the breach within 15 days of a written warning, with a refund of the proportional part of the sum. Either party may terminate the agreement on [[PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 30]] days' notice if the event is permanently cancelled.

### Article 13 — Other provisions

The agreement is governed by the law of Montenegro. The parties resolve disputes by agreement, and if that fails, the [[PLACEHOLDER: court]] has jurisdiction. Amendments to the agreement are valid only in writing. Annexes 1 (package and consideration), 2 (name and mark usage rules) and 3 (sponsor report) form part of the agreement. The agreement is made in two identical originals.

### Annex 2 — Name and mark usage rules

The sponsor may use: the association's mark and the event name in the form given in the brand manual the association supplies, without changes to colours, proportions or text, with clear space; the designation “[Official partner / Title partner / Partner] [event name and year]”; the photographs the association supplies under Article 6. The sponsor may not: alter the mark, combine it with its own into a single mark, use it on products for sale or in political messages, or use it in a way that suggests that the association endorses the sponsor's products or that the sponsor had a say in the choice of beneficiaries. The sponsor submits every piece of material carrying the association's mark to the association for approval at least [[PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 5]] working days before publication; the association responds within three working days.

### Annex 3 — Sponsor report

Within 30 days of the event, without being reminded, the association delivers to the sponsor a report with: photographs showing the sponsor's people and its branding, with the consent records; the number of participants, volunteers and visitors; an overview of media coverage and posts; the amount of funds raised by source; the beneficiaries to whom funds were awarded, in the form the Privacy Policy allows, with a link to the public ledger; the delivery status of every item in Annex 1; the date of the renewal conversation.

## PART B: SPONSORSHIP RULES (INTERNAL)

### Article 14 — Packages and price list

The Board adopts the sponsorship packages and price list for the year, on the proposal of the Executive Director. The starting proposal is the sheet from the team guide: Core Cost Partner [[PLACEHOLDER: amount, team guide: 10,000 EUR and above per year]]; Title Partner [[PLACEHOLDER: amount, team guide: 5,000 EUR per event]]; Gold [[PLACEHOLDER: amount, team guide: 2,500 EUR]]; Silver [[PLACEHOLDER: amount, team guide: 1,000 EUR]]; Local Business [[PLACEHOLDER: amount, team guide: 250 to 500 EUR]]; Match Partner (any amount, matching within a window); In kind (valued, recorded and recognised like cash). Every package must contain the three items in Article 2. Chapters sell sponsorships only under these packages, and the Executive Director may approve a deviation of up to [[PLACEHOLDER: percentage]] percent of the price, with reasons.

### Article 15 — Who negotiates, who signs, how it is invoiced

Negotiations are led by the Executive Director, the person in charge of partnerships and, within the packages, the chapter leads. Only the Executive Director signs the agreement; an agreement above [[PLACEHOLDER: amount]] EUR or with exclusivity is approved by the Board in advance. The association issues the invoice as instructed by the accountant, marked “sponsorship” and with the name of the event; payment goes only to the association's account and is booked to the Operations Fund, and the share belonging to the chapter under the published formula is recorded for that chapter. In-kind sponsorship is recorded with its value and published as an in-kind gift with the sponsor named.

### Article 16 — Sponsor screening

Before signing, the Executive Director checks: that the sponsor is not in an industry the Board has excluded [[PLACEHOLDER: list, e.g. tobacco, gambling, weapons; decided by the Board, see Donation Policy Art. 18]]; that there are no publicly known circumstances that would make the sponsorship harmful to the association's reputation; that the sponsor is not a connected person of a person covered by the Conflict of Interest Rules, and if it is, that the conflict has been declared and that person takes no part in the decision; that the sponsor accepts Article 3. Sponsorship from a political party, a candidate or a religious community is not accepted. Sponsorship from alcohol brands is accepted only for adult events and without branding of children's zones or children's materials.

The tax relief the team guide cites as a selling point (a deduction of up to 3.5 percent of gross income for humanitarian, sports, health, cultural and environmental giving) is used in conversations with sponsors only once the accountant has confirmed it in writing and it is clear whether it applies to sponsorship or only to donations; until then it is not mentioned.

### Article 17 — Delivery and records

The person in charge of partnerships, or the Executive Director until one is appointed, is responsible for the delivery of every item in Annex 1 and keeps the sponsor record on the platform (name, tier, amount or value, in kind, chapter, contract, items and delivery status, status: prospect, in negotiation, signed, active, completed). The report in Annex 3 is sent within 30 days without being reminded; a late report is logged as a failure. The renewal conversation is scheduled before expiry. Sponsors are published on the partners page and in the annual report by tier.
