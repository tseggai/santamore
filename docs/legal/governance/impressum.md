# Impressum / Podaci o organizaciji / Organisation Details

Full draft of the page behind `/impressum` (`content/legal/impressum.ts`), the page the card acquirer inspects before enabling live payments (brief § legal pages). Unlike the other governance documents this one is almost entirely facts, and every fact is a placeholder until the registration steps produce it; the document's value is that it names every field the page must carry, where each value comes from, and what else an acquirer looks for across the site. Montenegrin operative text first, English under each block. Drafted 2026-09-21 from the site draft, the brief and the registration pack. **No value here may be invented**; the fill-in table in Part C says which document or step supplies each one.

### 6. Kuda ide novac / Where the money goes

Vodimo dva odvojena fonda: donacije idu u Fond za pomoć i sto posto neto iznosa isplaćuje se korisnicima; kotizacije, sponzorstva, grantovi i članarine idu u Operativni fond, iz kojeg se plaća rad organizacije. O tome ko dobija sredstva odlučuje nezavisna Komisija za dodjelu sredstava po objavljenim kriterijumima. Svaka uplata i isplata objavljena je u javnom registru na stranici Transparentnost, a godišnji finansijski izvještaj na stranici [[PLACEHOLDER: putanja]].

*Two separate funds, one hundred percent of net donations to beneficiaries, the independent Grants Committee deciding under published criteria, every payment published in the ledger, the annual financial report on a named page.*

*The authorised representative is responsible for the site's content; photos and texts belong to the association or are used with permission; no use of the name, mark or content without consent; no liability for linked sites; content complaints to the contact email, answered within 15 days.*

### 8. Nadzor i rješavanje sporova / Supervision and disputes

Nadzor nad radom nevladinih organizacija vrši Ministarstvo iz tačke 1; nadzor nad zaštitom podataka Agencija za zaštitu ličnih podataka i slobodan pristup informacijama Crne Gore. Sporove sa donatorima i učesnicima nastojimo riješiti dogovorom preko kontakata iz tačke 3; mjerodavno je pravo Crne Gore.

*Supervision by the registering ministry and, for data, the Agency for Personal Data Protection; disputes settled by agreement where possible, Montenegrin law applies.*

## DIO C: ODAKLE DOLAZI SVAKI PODATAK / PART C: WHERE EACH VALUE COMES FROM

Every placeholder above is supplied by a registration step or a contract. Fill the page only from these sources, then update `docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md` and the footer keys in `messages/*.json`.

| Podatak / Value | Izvor / Source | Korak / Step |
|---|---|---|
| Registered name, short name / Naziv | Decision on founding Art. 2; Statute Art. 2; registration decision | Founding assembly; Ministry decision |
| Registered address / Adresa | Decision on founding Art. 3; registration decision | Founding assembly |
| Registration number and date, registering body / Registarski broj | Decision on registration (rješenje o registraciji) | Ministry, within 10 days of a complete application |
| Authorised representative / Ovlašćeno lice | Decision on founding Art. 7; Minutes; registration decision | Founding assembly |
| Bodies / Organi | Statute Art. 9 as finally adopted | Founding assembly |
| MONSTAT activity code / Šifra djelatnosti | Notice of classification (obavještenje o razvrstavanju) | MONSTAT, after the registration decision |
| PIB | Certificate from the Tax Administration | Tax Administration, after MONSTAT |
| VAT status / PDV | Accountant's confirmation; tax registration | With the PIB |
| CRPS entry / Upis u CRPS | CRPS excerpt | Only if an economic activity is registered |
| Bank, account name, IBAN, BIC / Banka | Account-opening contract; bank's written confirmation of IBAN and BIC | Bank, after the PIB and the OP form |
| Processor legal name, acquiring bank, accepted brands, descriptor, 3-D Secure programme names, logo artwork / Procesor, brendovi, descriptor | Monri merchant contract and onboarding pack | Monri, after the bank account |
| Emails, phone, hours, domain / Kontakti | Board decision; domain registration; email provider setup | Before go-live |
| Annual report page / Stranica izvještaja | Site route once built | Before the first annual report |

The JSON-LD `Organization` block on the site should carry the same legal name, address, email and phone as this page. Add the card brand logos to `public/brand/` only from the schemes' official artwork with their required clear space and minimum size. The page `content/legal/impressum.ts` is regenerated from Part A once the values exist; the acquirer checklist in Part B is a go-live gate, not page content.
