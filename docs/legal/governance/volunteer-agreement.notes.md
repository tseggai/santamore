# Volunteer Agreement and Volunteer Rules — notes

## 1. What the document is and where it hangs

The agreement every volunteer accepts and the rules behind it. It is the document the Terms of Use (Art. 9), the Code of Conduct (Art. 10), the Child Safeguarding Policy (Art. 9), the Conflict of Interest Policy and the Chapter Rules refer to.

- **Part A** is the short form an event-day volunteer accepts on the platform when signing up (and again when a task for a specific event is confirmed).
- **Part B** is the full written agreement for recurring roles: team leads, club connectors, chapter officers (chapter lead, treasurer, volunteer coordinator), safeguarding leads and anyone with regular tasks. Annex 1 (role description) is part of it; Annexes 2 (declaration of interests) and 3 (safeguarding declaration) come from the Conflict of Interest Policy and the Child Safeguarding Policy.
- **Part C** is the volunteer rules that apply to both.

Montenegrin is the operative text; the English file is a translation. Drafted 2026-09-21 from the team guide (roles and their time cost), the brief and the governance drafts. **The lawyer reviews against Montenegro's Law on Volunteering before use**, in particular which volunteering must have a written contract, what it must contain, insurance duties and the rules for minors; nothing in the draft states those as fact.

## 2. Decisions embedded in the draft

From the team guide and the policies:

- Volunteering is unpaid; justified, pre-approved costs are reimbursed against receipts (claim within 30 days, paid within 15).
- Every volunteer is briefed before every event (at least 15 minutes); no child, water or cash task without the briefing.
- Child-facing tasks need the safeguarding declaration (and, for permanent roles, training and a criminal record certificate).
- Cash is handled in pairs, against a receipt, and handed to the treasurer immediately.
- Volunteers wear the badge only on duty and represent the association only within their task; no statements to media or in the association's name without written authority.
- Minors (15 to 18, age to be confirmed by the lawyer) volunteer only with a parent's or guardian's confirmation and never with money, on the water or alone with children.
- Water tasks only for volunteers designated by the national safety lead, with lifeguards present.
- Either side may end a Part B agreement in writing with a notice period; the association may end it without notice for breaches of the Code of Conduct, safeguarding, conflict-of-interest or money rules, after hearing the volunteer under the Code's procedure.
- The association may reassign a volunteer away from child contact without giving reasons and without consequence.
- Materials made in the role may be used by the association without payment or time limit; the volunteer keeps authorship and portfolio use; photo consent is a choice, changeable at any time.

## 3. Checks, parameters and platform notes (Part D of the draft)

**CHECK with the lawyer:** the Law on Volunteering's rules on when a written contract is mandatory (threshold in hours or duration), the mandatory contents of the contract and whether Part B covers them all, the minimum age for minors and the form of parental consent, the accident-insurance duty and who bears it, permitted reimbursements and allowances and their tax treatment, the liability allocation in Article 7, any register or reporting duty towards the authorities for volunteers, and whether the event-day form in Part A suffices for one-off volunteering or a short written contract is still required.

**CHECK with the insurer:** a policy covering volunteers for accident and liability during activities, including water and children's activities.

**CHECK with the accountant:** how reimbursements are documented and booked.

Suggested parameters, each a single line to change:

| Parameter | Suggested | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Written agreement threshold (prag za pisani ugovor) | per the Law; if none, any role above 10 hours a month or 3 months | Part B intro |
| Meals reimbursed on tasks longer than (ishrana na zadatku dužem od) | 5 hours | Art. 5 |
| Retention of volunteer data after the end (čuvanje podataka) | 2 years | Art. 8 |
| Notice period (otkazni rok) | 15 days | Art. 12 |
| Minimum age with parental consent (minimalni uzrast) | per the Law, suggested 15 | Art. 14 |
| Task confirmation before an event (potvrda zadatka) | 7 days | Art. 15 |

**Platform notes.** The volunteer sign-up page exists; it should collect what Part A needs (role preference, emergency contact, shirt size, photo choice, the declarations with version and timestamp, and a parent's confirmation for minors) and write to the volunteer register the Chapter Rules and safeguarding policy expect (roles, briefings with date and briefer, hours, declarations, certificates for permanent roles). Staff records in the admin console already hold volunteers as team records; the register fields above extend them. A certificate of volunteering can be generated from the register. Part B agreements are documents in storage linked to the staff record, with the accepted version noted.

## 5. Montenegrin corrections and open doubts

Corrections made to the operative text (old → new):

- Part A, item 4: "nije vođen ni se ne vodi postupak" → "nije vođen niti se vodi postupak" (double negation in the coordinated clause).
- Art. 8: "ne fotografiše ni ne objavljuje" → "ne fotografiše niti objavljuje" (same construction).
- Art. 17: "Volonteri koji uzmu istaknute uloge" → "Volonteri koji preuzmu istaknute uloge" (verb choice for taking on a role).

Open doubts:

- Art. 8, "[[PLACEHOLDER: broj]] godine": the noun ending depends on the number chosen (2 to 4 → "godine", 5 or more → "godina"); adjust when the parameter is set.
- Art. 10, "ne prima poklone iznad simbolične vrijednosti ni novac ni od koga u vezi sa radom udruženja": left as drafted; the lawyer may prefer to restate it as two clauses (no gifts above token value; no money from anyone).
- The Part B parties clause uses the long form "Nevladino udruženje „Santamore“"; the registered name is "SANTAMORE NVU" and the lawyer may want the registered form in the agreement header.
- "Pravila o sukobu interesa" and "Pravila o ograncima" are the names the draft uses for the Conflict of Interest Policy and the Chapter Rules; they were kept as the other drafts use them.
- Terminology "organizator volontiranja" (volunteering organiser) follows the usual Law on Volunteering wording for the association as a party; the lawyer confirms it matches the statute's definition.
