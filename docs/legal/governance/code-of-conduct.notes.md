# Code of Conduct — notes

## 1. What the document is and where it hangs

Full draft of the code behind the public page at `/kodeks` (`content/legal/codeOfConduct.ts`). The site page is a short, warm summary and stays that way; this document is the complete code the Board adopts, which the Terms of Use (Art. 6 and 10), the Event Terms (Art. 8), the Chapter Rules and the Child Safeguarding Policy all refer to, plus the internal incident procedure behind it (Part B). Montenegrin is the operative text; the English and Russian files are translations. Drafted 2026-09-21 from the site draft, the team guide (tone: first person plural, concrete, no jargon), the brief and the other governance drafts. **The lawyer reviews Part B and the sanctions before adoption.** Bracketed values are parameters; suggested values are in section 3 below.

## 2. Decisions embedded in the draft

From the site draft and the team guide: everyone is welcome and nobody gets mocked; harassment is not tolerated; alcohol only for adults and never before or during sport; the safeguarding policy governs anything involving children; safety instructions are followed; reports are confidential and never punished; sanctions run from a warning to a ban.

## 5. Montenegrin corrections made and open doubts

Corrections (old → new):

- Art. 1: "Prihvatanjem uslova učešća" → "Prihvatanjem Uslova učešća" (document title, capitalised consistently with "Uslovima korišćenja" in Art. 4 and 14 and the term list).
- Art. 14 table: the bilingual "Mjera / Measure", "Kada / When" header and the bilingual cells were split into their languages (no wording change).

Otherwise none: the Montenegrin text was copied as drafted; no substance, numbers, thresholds or deadlines were changed.

Doubts:

- The Montenegrin mixes second person singular ("startuj", "prati", "obrati se") and plural ("prihvatate", "pišite", "možete") across articles. This looks like a deliberate tone choice from the site draft (informal where it addresses an individual participant, formal where it addresses the reader as user or member), so it was left as is. The Russian translation uses the polite plural ("вы") throughout, which is the natural register in Russian; the English is neutral.
- Art. 10 lists "lica ogranaka" (chapter persons) without using the statute's "chapter lead" term; translated literally as "chapter persons" / "лица отделений". The lawyer may want to align it with the Chapter Rules' wording.
- Art. 10 and 17 refer to "Pravila o sukobu interesa" (Conflict of Interest Policy) and Art. 16 to the "Paket za ogranak" (Chapter Pack), which are not in the agreed term list; translated as "Conflict of Interest Policy" / "Правила о конфликте интересов" and "Chapter Pack" / "Комплект для отделения".
- The Executive Director is referred to as "on" (he) in Art. 10 and 17 of the Montenegrin; the English uses "they", the Russian keeps the grammatical masculine, which is standard for the office in Russian legal text.
