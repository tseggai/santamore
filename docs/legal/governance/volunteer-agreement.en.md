### Article 1 — Subject

The volunteer undertakes to perform volunteer activities for the volunteering organiser in the role of [role name], described in Annex 1 (role description), voluntarily and without pay, in order to further the association's goals. Volunteering does not establish an employment relationship and creates no right to a salary, remuneration for work or other rights arising from employment.

### Article 2 — Duration and scope

The agreement is concluded for [[PLACEHOLDER: a fixed term until a date / an indefinite term]]. The expected scope of the engagement is about [number] hours a month, as agreed with the [national contact / volunteer coordinator / Executive Director]; the volunteer is not obliged to accept a task they cannot perform and informs the coordinator of this as early as possible.

### Article 3 — Duties of the volunteering organiser

The volunteering organiser undertakes to: provide the volunteer with the induction and training the role requires, including training in the Child Safeguarding Policy where the role involves it; provide the conditions, equipment, materials and instructions for safe work; name a person who is the volunteer's permanent contact; reimburse justified costs under Article 5; provide the insurance under Article 6; process the volunteer's data under the Privacy Policy; issue on request a certificate of volunteering with the role description, duration and number of hours; treat the volunteer with respect, protect them from harassment and hear their suggestions and complaints.

### Article 4 — Duties of the volunteer

The volunteer undertakes to: perform tasks conscientiously and according to instructions, within the role under Annex 1; complete the induction and training; comply with the Statute, the Code of Conduct, the Child Safeguarding Policy, the Conflict of Interest Policy, the Chapter Rules and the other acts of the association that apply to the role, and sign the declarations those acts require; keep the confidentiality of data under Article 8; not conclude contracts, assume obligations, receive sponsorships or donations into a personal account or make public statements on behalf of the association without written authorisation; handle the association's money and property only under the cash and expense rules; report conflicts of interest, incidents and breaches of the rules; keep a record of hours in the manner the coordinator sets; return equipment, materials and access credentials on termination of the agreement.

### Article 5 — Expenses

The volunteer is entitled to reimbursement of justified costs incurred in connection with volunteering (travel, materials, communication, meals on a task longer than [[PLACEHOLDER: number]] hours) which the coordinator approved in advance, against a receipt and a claim submitted within 30 days; the association reimburses within 15 days of a complete claim. Reimbursement of costs is not remuneration for work. Lump-sum allowances are not paid, unless the law expressly permits them and the Board so decides, with the accountant's written opinion. ### Article 8 — Confidentiality and data

The volunteer keeps confidential all data about donors, beneficiaries, participants, children, members, sponsors, employees and the internal work of the association that they come across in the role, during and after the termination of the agreement, and uses it only for the task. Access to the association's systems is used only personally and is revoked on the day the agreement ends. The volunteer does not photograph or publish participants, beneficiaries or children with private devices or accounts. The volunteer's data is processed under the Privacy Policy and kept for [[PLACEHOLDER: number]] years after the agreement ends.

### Article 9 — Working with children

If the role involves working with children, before the first task the volunteer signs the declaration under the Child Safeguarding Policy, completes the training and, for the permanent roles under that policy, provides a criminal record certificate [[PLACEHOLDER: legal basis, Child Safeguarding Policy, part IX]]. The association may, without giving reasons and without consequences for the volunteer, assign the volunteer to tasks without contact with children.

### Article 10 — Conflicts of interest, gifts and media

The Conflict of Interest Policy applies to the volunteer: they declare interests and conflicts, do not accept gifts above token value or money from anyone in connection with the association's work, and do not take part in decisions in which they have an interest. They do not make statements to the media on policy, finances, beneficiaries or incidents; they forward enquiries to the Executive Director the same day. On their own accounts they clearly speak in their own name.

### Article 11 — Intellectual property and photographs

The association may use the materials the volunteer creates in the role (texts, photographs, design, lists, plans) for its work without payment or time limit, crediting the author where customary; the volunteer retains authorship and the right to use them in their portfolio without data about persons. At signing, the volunteer chooses whether they consent to being recognisable in published photographs and on the team page; the choice may be changed.

### Article 12 — Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement by written notice, with a notice period of [[PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 15]] days, without giving reasons. The association may terminate the agreement without a notice period in the event of a breach of the Code of Conduct, the Child Safeguarding Policy, the Conflict of Interest Policy or the rules on money, after hearing the volunteer under the procedure in the Code. The agreement also ends on expiry of its term, when the role ends or by mutual agreement. On termination, the volunteer returns the equipment, materials, badge and access credentials within 15 days, and the association issues on request the certificate under Article 3.

## PART C: VOLUNTEER RULES

### Article 14 — Who may volunteer

An adult may volunteer and, with the written consent of a parent or guardian, so may a person aged [[PLACEHOLDER: age, per the Law on Volunteering, e.g. 15]] or over, on age-appropriate tasks, without money, water or unsupervised work with children. Foreign nationals volunteer under the same conditions [[PLACEHOLDER: confirm whether residence status restricts volunteering]]. A person finally convicted of an offence against children may not volunteer on any task.

### Article 15 — Sign-up, rostering and briefing

Sign-up is through the site or through the chapter's volunteer coordinator and is entered in the volunteer register (name, contact, roles, briefings, hours, declarations). The coordinator assigns tasks, confirms them to the volunteer at least [[PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 7]] days before the event with the place, time, duration and contact, and holds a briefing of at least 15 minutes before every event (roles, safety, child safeguarding, cash, whom to report a problem to). A volunteer who has not attended the briefing is not given a task with children, on the water or with money.

### Article 16 — On duty

The volunteer wears the badge, arrives on time, reports to the coordinator on arrival and departure, stays at the assigned post until relieved, does not consume alcohol, follows instructions and reports problems. Tasks on the water are performed only by volunteers designated by the national safety lead, with lifeguards present. Tasks with cash are performed by two volunteers together. The volunteer does not transport participants or children in their own vehicle except under the Child Safeguarding Policy and with the coordinator's approval.

### Article 17 — Recognition and support

The association provides volunteers with: a T-shirt or badge, refreshments on the task, public thanks, a certificate of volunteering on request, an invitation to the annual volunteer gathering and, where possible, training that is useful to the volunteer beyond the association. Volunteer hours are entered in the association's annual report as the community's contribution. Volunteers who take on prominent roles may be proposed for the association's bodies under the Statute. The association takes care not to ask the same people for everything: the coordinator monitors workload and offers a break before anyone asks for one.

### Article 18 — Volunteer complaints

The volunteer may report any problem, including the conduct of other volunteers, the team or participants, to the coordinator, the event manager, the Executive Director or, if it concerns them, the President of the Board, under the Code of Conduct. The report is confidential and must have no consequences for the volunteer. The volunteer has the right to be heard before any measure that concerns them.
