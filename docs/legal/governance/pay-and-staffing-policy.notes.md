# Pay and staffing policy — notes

## What the document is and where it hangs

The Board's policy on employment and pay, drawn from the team guide's "being honest about salaries" rules, its phase plan and the loaded-cost arithmetic, and from Article 30 of the Law on NGOs as the Ministry's guidance note summarises it. It carries figures, so its audience is the Executive Director and the administrators only (manifest `audience: leadership`; the console shows it to the `director` and `admin` roles). Montenegrin (`pay-and-staffing-policy.me.md`) is the operative text; `pay-and-staffing-policy.en.md` is the translation. The accountant reviews Articles 2 and 5 before the first payroll.

## Decisions embedded in the draft

- The six rules from the team guide, including staff at roughly 35 percent of revenue and hiring behind the Operations Fund.
- Only independent Board members vote on pay; the Director never on their own.
- A founder's past time is a contribution, not a claim; a founder's documented spending is a justified cost only with the Board's approval, paid from operating income; a founder's loan is a loan.
- The phase plan and the loaded cost (€1,200 to €1,600 a month per role) are the team guide's 2026 figures.
- Pay bands are placeholders until benchmarked; the part-time coordinator figure (€500 to €800 for three months) is the team guide's.
