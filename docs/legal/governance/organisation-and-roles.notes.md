# Organisation and roles — notes

## What the document is and where it hangs

The members' description of how Santamore is organised: the bodies from the statute template and the statute additions, the decision-rights matrix, the hierarchy and the tie-break rules from section 6 of `docs/legal/registration/statute-additions.md` (adopted by the founders 2026-10-21 and merged into form 04), the volunteer roles from the team guide, and membership (how to join, rights, duties, the 2026 fee waiver, the benefits the Board will put to the Assembly for 2027). Montenegrin (`organisation-and-roles.me.md`) is the operative text; `organisation-and-roles.en.md` is the translation. Audience: every signed-in member (the member area) and the staff (the console); the org chart (`components/org/OrgChart.tsx`) is drawn above it in both places.

## Decisions embedded in the draft

- The founder who started Santamore, Tseggai Debrezion, is the first Executive Director and President of the Assembly, as the founding minutes of 26 September 2026 record; Dragana Durica is deputy President.
- Decision rights follow the delegation-of-authority pattern: a short list of matters reserved to the Assembly, the Board and the Grants Committee, everything else delegated to the Executive Director within the budget.
- Tie-breaks: the President of the Assembly and the President of the Board have a casting vote except on pay, dismissals and the Director's report; an inquorate or tied Board hands an operational matter to the Director.
- No fee for 2026; a yearly Assembly decision from 2027, with the benefits list.
- Head counts (forty event volunteers, twenty fundraisers, about five team leads, three officers per chapter) are the team guide's targets.
