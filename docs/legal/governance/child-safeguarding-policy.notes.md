# Child Safeguarding Policy — notes

## 1. What the document is and where it hangs

Internal policy that Article 34 of the statute additions (`docs/legal/registration/statute-additions.md`) has the Board adopt ("pravila o zaštiti djece i ranjivih lica"). The public page at `/zastita-djece` (`content/legal/safeguarding.ts`) states the promises to parents; this document is the operating policy behind it and must never promise less. It is referenced by the Chapter Rules (Art. 12 and 21), the grant criteria (section 12) and the event terms. Montenegrin is the operative text. Drafted 2026-09-21. **The lawyer reviews before adoption**, in particular reporting duties and vetting (the checks below, referenced in the document as "Part IX"). Bracketed values are parameters for the Board; suggested values are below.

The draft was titled "Politika zaštite djece i ranjivih lica / Child and Vulnerable Adult Safeguarding Policy"; the published files carry the short title "Politika zaštite djece" / "Child Safeguarding Policy" / "Политика защиты детей" as instructed. The draft's Part IX ("Final provisions, parameters and checks") is reduced in the document to the final provisions (Article 26) and renamed "IX. Final provisions"; the parameters, checks and platform notes are in sections 3 and 4 here.

## 2. Decisions embedded in the draft

From the team guide and the public page: no unsupervised adult-child contact; every volunteer briefed; parental consent for identifiable photos; a named safeguarding lead at every event; the child's welfare before programme, photo, result or schedule.

## 3. Parameters for the Board and platform notes

Suggested starting values, each a single line to change:

|---|---|---|
| Training for safeguarding leads and chapter officers (Obuka) | 3 hours on appointment, yearly refresher | Art. 10 |
| Adult-to-child ratio (Odnos odraslih i djece) | 1 : 8 up to age 8, 1 : 12 above | Art. 11 |
| Parent present at the event (Prisustvo roditelja) | under 12 | Art. 12 |

| Retention of children's event data (Čuvanje podataka) | 6 months | Art. 16 |
| Retention of the reports register (Evidencija prijava) | 10 years | Art. 22 |

Other placeholders in the document: the legal basis and scope of the criminal record certificate (Art. 9, see check 1 below); the police, ambulance and Tivat Centre for Social Work numbers (Art. 20, see check 3 below); the additional retention period of the reports register after an official proceeding (Art. 22).

**Platform notes.** Registration already collects the waiver and photo consent per participant; for children it should record the registering parent, a reachable phone number, the named collecting adult (Art. 12) and any health note (Art. 16). The event page already publishes the safeguarding lead's name. The volunteer register should record briefings (Art. 10). The public page `content/legal/safeguarding.ts` should be updated to name the national lead and reporting contacts once appointed.

## 4. Checks and points for the lawyer

1. **CHECK with the lawyer:** the legal basis for requesting a criminal record certificate from volunteers and staff (Art. 9) and whether it may be limited to specific offences; the association's duty to report suspected abuse to the centre for social work or the police under Montenegrin family and child-protection law, so that Article 21 states it exactly; processing of children's data and of reports under the Law on Personal Data Protection, including retention.
2. **CHECK with the national safety lead and the insurer:** lifeguard numbers and water-zone rules for children (Art. 14) against the policy's conditions.
3. **CHECK with the Centre for Social Work in Tivat:** their contact and preferred reporting route, for Article 20 and the Chapter in a Box.

Note for the lawyer: the placeholder in Article 9 still reads "dio IX" (Part IX) because the draft kept its checks in Part IX; in the published document Part IX now holds only the final provisions and the check itself is item 1 above. Reword or drop the reference when the placeholder is resolved.

## 5. Montenegrin corrections made

- Part IX heading: "ZAVRŠNE ODREDBE, PARAMETRI I PROVJERE" → "ZAVRŠNE ODREDBE" (parameters and checks moved here).
- Art. 8: "postupa po dijelu VI" → "postupa po dijelu VII" (reporting is Part VII; Part VI is photos, data and communication; the draft's English summary carried the same wrong reference).
- Art. 9: "nije vođen ni se ne vodi postupak" → "nije vođen niti se vodi postupak".
- Art. 11: the list of what the risk assessment contains put in the accusative throughout: "tačka okupljanja …, prva pomoć, sanitarni uslovi i posebni rizici" → "tačku okupljanja …, prvu pomoć, sanitarne uslove i posebne rizike".

- Art. 23: the sentence had no verb: "Kršenje ove politike, srazmjerno težini: upozorenje i ponovna obuka; …" → "Kršenje ove politike povlači, srazmjerno težini: upozorenje i ponovnu obuku; …".

## 6. Doubts

- Art. 12: replacing "izjava o odricanju od odgovornosti" with the defined term "Izjava o učešću" assumes the draft meant the Participation Waiver of the event terms and not a separate instrument; the substance (the parent accepts the terms and the waiver) is unchanged.
- Art. 8: the change of the cross-reference from Part VI to Part VII is a substantive correction of an evident slip; the lawyer should confirm.
- Art. 14 speaks of a "nacionalno lice za bezbjednost" (national safety lead), a role defined in the volunteer agreement and chapter rules drafts, not in this policy; kept as written.
- Art. 9 placeholder still points to "Part IX" for the check (see the note in section 4).
- "Lice za zaštitu" is rendered "safeguarding lead" (EN) and "ответственный за защиту" (RU); "obveznik" as "covered person" / "обязанное лицо"; "Paket za ogranak" as "Chapter in a Box" / "Пакет для отделения"; "Kriterijumi za dodjelu sredstava" as "Grant Criteria" / "Критерии распределения средств".
