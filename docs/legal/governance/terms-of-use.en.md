### Article 4 — Fundraiser page

When you open a fundraiser page, you raise funds for the association and for the cause the page is linked to; you do not receive money yourself. All payments go to the association's account, are recorded in the public ledger and are shown on your page. The page must not direct donors to any other means of payment or to other accounts.

You are responsible for the content of the page: its title, story, photograph and target. The rules are simple:

1. write truthfully; do not promise what the cause does not cover and do not present yourself as the beneficiary if you are not;
2. publish only photographs and texts for which you hold the rights and the consent of the people shown; for children the Child Safeguarding Policy applies;
3. no offensive, discriminatory, political, religious or unlawful content, and no third-party advertising;
4. if you are raising for a cause whose beneficiary is a person connected to you, say so on the page;
5. record on the site any cash you collect in person and hand it over to the association under the cash rules; until receipt is confirmed, the amount is shown as “handed in, pending”.

You may use the sharing material the site generates (images with your name, photograph and total) only to promote your own page, without altering the association's mark.

You may close your page at any time; the amount raised stays with the association and the cause. A page closes automatically [[PLACEHOLDER: number]] days after the event or cause for which it was opened, and its final total remains publicly visible.

The team total is the sum of its members' pages; the internal ranking of members is visible to team members and publicly, unless a member hides their name. A member may leave the team at any time; their total stays credited to the team up to the moment they leave.
