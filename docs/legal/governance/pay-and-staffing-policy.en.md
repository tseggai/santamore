# Pay and staffing policy

## I. PURPOSE AND ACCESS

### Article 1 — Purpose

This policy sets out how Santamore decides who is employed, how pay is set, what a founder may and may not be paid for, and how the team is meant to grow. It carries the planning figures and the pay bands, so it is read by the Executive Director and the administrators only. The organisation document, available to every member, describes the roles without the figures.

### Article 2 — Legal frame

Article 30 of the Law on Non-Governmental Organisations allows the association's assets to be used only to achieve its goals, to reimburse justified costs incurred in achieving them, to pay salaries, remuneration and other earnings of employees on the basis of work, and to give occasional awards in accordance with the statute. The assets may not be distributed among the founders, members, the bodies or the employees.

That prohibits distributing surplus. It does not prohibit a market salary for work actually done. Because founders and staff sit in the same clause, the difference must be obvious and documented for every payment to a founder.

## II. THE RULES

### Article 3 — Six rules

1. Contracts, not transfers: every paid person has a written contract, a job description, documented hours and payroll with taxes paid. No exceptions, not even for founders.
2. The payer is not the payee: anyone taking pay cannot be in the Board majority that sets it. The independent members are recruited before the first hire.
3. Bands published: the pay bands go in the annual report, and the benchmark used goes in the minutes.
4. No relatives on the payroll in the early years. Even where legal, it is the first thing a critic reaches for.
5. A written opinion first: from a Montenegrin accountant, before the first payroll.
6. Staff at roughly 35 percent of revenue. We hire behind the Operations Fund, never ahead of it.

### Article 4 — Who decides pay

The Board adopts the staffing act, decides every hire and dismissal and sets pay. Only the independent members of the Board vote on anyone's pay; a member who is paid by the association, or whose relative is, leaves the room for that item.

The Executive Director proposes the pay of staff with a written justification and benchmark data, and never takes part in setting their own pay. Pay bands, not individual figures, are published in the annual report.

### Article 5 — Founders and pay

A founder may be paid under the same rules as anyone else, for work actually done under a contract, in a role the Board has created in the staffing act. The pay of a founder who is Executive Director is set by the independent members of the Board on a written benchmark.

Time a founder gave before the Operations Fund existed is a contribution to the association and not a claim against it. Money a founder spent on the association's behalf is reimbursed as a justified cost only where it is documented, was approved by the Board in advance or recognised by the Board in writing afterwards, and is paid from operating income, never from donations. A loan from a founder is recorded as a loan, with its terms in the minutes, and repaid only by Board decision.

## III. THE PLAN

### Article 6 — Staffing plan

The plan is the team guide's, phase by phase. Loaded cost is roughly €1,200 to €1,600 a month per full-time role, so one person needs about €60,000 to €80,000 of operating revenue a year behind them.

| Phase | When | Paid roles | Operating revenue needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prove it | Now to January 2027 | All volunteer, plus one part-time event coordinator paid from sponsorship, €500 to €800 a month for three months | €35,000 to €60,000 |
| Institutionalise | 2027 | Executive Director (1.0), Events and Chapters (0.5 growing to 1.0) | €90,000 to €130,000 |
| Scale | 2028 | Plus Partnerships (1.0) and Communications (0.5) | €180,000 to €240,000 |
| Institution | 2029 onwards | Five to six full time; chapter leads part-paid | €300,000 and above |

The Executive Director is the first hire, before a marketer: the year-one bottleneck is execution and sponsor relationships, not awareness.

### Article 8 — Hiring procedure

1. The Board creates the position in the staffing act, with a job description and a band.
2. The position is advertised openly; a founder or a current volunteer applies like anyone else.
3. The Executive Director, or the Board for the Director's own position, interviews and recommends.
4. The independent members of the Board decide, with the benchmark recorded in the minutes.
5. A written contract is signed before the first day of work.
6. The band is published in the next annual report.

### Article 9 — Personal data

The pay of a named person is personal data. Contracts, payslips and the individual figures are kept by the Executive Director and the accountant, and are shown to the Board only for the decision that needs them. This policy carries bands, not names.

## IV. FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 10 — Review

The Board reviews this policy with every annual budget, and whenever a phase of the plan is reached or the Law changes.
