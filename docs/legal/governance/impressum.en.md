## 6. Where the money goes

We keep two separate funds: donations, entry fees and grants go to the Impact Fund, from which one hundred percent of the net amount is paid out to beneficiaries; sponsorship goes to the Operations Fund, which pays for the work of the organisation. Who receives funds is decided by the independent Grants Committee under published criteria. Every payment in and out is published in the public ledger on the Transparency page, and the annual financial report on the page [[PLACEHOLDER: path]].
