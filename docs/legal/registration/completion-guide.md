# Registration pack: completion guide / Uputstvo za popunjavanje

Field-by-field instructions for the four documents Santamore files with the Ministry of Regional-Investment Development and Cooperation with NGOs (MIRN) to register as a non-governmental association (*nevladino udruženje*). English first, Montenegrin second in every row. Prepared 2026-09-21 from the seven documents the team received: the five templates and the two official guidance notes (translated as documents 6 and 7). Where a guidance note settles a point, the row says **confirmed** and cites it.

**Nothing here is legal advice.** Values marked **[[PLACEHOLDER]]** are facts the founders must supply. Items marked **CHECK** are points to confirm with the Ministry contact (Milan Šestović, 068/878-096, milan.sestovic@mirn.gov.me) or the lawyer drafting the statute. Suggested Montenegrin wording is a draft for the lawyer to review, not final text.

## 2. Document 1: Instructions / Instrukcije za osnivanje NVO

No fields to fill. It is the roadmap. Documents 6 and 7 (the official guidance notes on the founding decision and the statute) also have no fields; their rules are folded into the rows below. Two corrections to how you read the roadmap:

- **Order of steps.** Step 4 (bank) requires the PIB from step 5. Real order: Ministry → seal → MONSTAT → Tax Administration (PIB) → bank → then Monri. / Korak 4 (banka) traži PIB iz koraka 5. Stvarni redosljed: Ministarstvo → pečat → MONSTAT → Poreska uprava (PIB) → banka → pa Monri.
- **"MJU" and "Ministarstvo" are the same ministry.** MJU was the former name (Ministry of Public Administration); the register now sits at MIRN. / „MJU“ i „Ministarstvo“ su isto ministarstvo pod starim i novim nazivom.
- **Step 3 (CRPS) is optional** and only needed if you register an economic activity. Recommended: skip it at founding, decide with the accountant before the first merchandise sale. / Korak 3 (CRPS) je opcion. Preporuka: preskočiti pri osnivanju, odlučiti sa računovođom prije prve prodaje.

What to bring to the Ministry (step 1) / Šta ponijeti u Ministarstvo:

- Application for entry, signed / Prijava za upis, potpisana
- Decision on founding, signed by all founders, the representative and the Chair / Odluka o osnivanju, potpisana od svih osnivača, ovlašćenog lica i Predsjedavajućeg
- Minutes of the founding assembly, signed / Zapisnik sa osnivačke skupštine, potpisan
- Statute, signed by the Chair; recommended that all founders sign the last page too / Statut, potpisan od Predsjedavajućeg; preporuka da svi osnivači potpišu i posljednju stranu
- Photocopies of every founder's ID card (passport for foreigners) / Fotokopije ličnih karata svih osnivača (pasoš za strance)
- **CHECK** how many copies of each the registry office wants; the instructions do not say. / **PROVJERITI** koliko primjeraka arhiva traži; instrukcije ne kažu.

**Structural gap to decide before filing.** The template has only two bodies: the Assembly and the Executive Director. The team guide commits to a Board of 5 to 7, an independent Grants Committee, and chapters. Two options:

Registered once, no amendment procedure. **Confirmed (document 7):** the Law allows additional bodies such as a Management Board, Executive Board or Supervisory Board, provided the statute states each body's powers, composition, election and dismissal, term, quorum and majority.
