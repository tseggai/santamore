# Guidance on completing the founding act (Decision on founding) of an association

English translation of *Uputstvo za popunjavanje osnivačkog akta (Odluke o osnivanju) udruženja*. Translation only; editorial notes are marked **[Translator's note]**.

Pursuant to Article 11, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Law on Non-Governmental Organisations, the founding act of an association contains:

- the personal names, or the names, of the founders;
- the unique personal identification number (JMB) of the founder, or the travel document (passport) number and the name of the state that issued it if the founder is a foreign citizen;
- the tax identification number (PIB) and the seal of the legal person, if the founder is a legal person;
- the signatures of the founders;
- the name, seat and address of the association;
- the goals and activities of the association;
- the personal name, unique personal identification number, address and signature of the person authorised to represent the association, or the travel document number and the name of the state that issued the travel document for founders who are foreign citizens.

A legal person as founder signs in such a way that its authorised person adds, next to the name of the legal person, their own signature, the tax identification number (PIB) and the seal of the legal person.

If the founder is a legal person, the data are entered as follows.

Example:

Article 1

The founders are:

1) name ____________, signature ___________, PIB ___________, seal _____________

When preparing the founding act, care should be taken, among other things, that the name of the association is stated precisely, exactly as it is to be entered in the register, and that it is written in the same way throughout the entire documentation. The goals and activities of the association stated in the founding act must be identical to those stated in the statute of the association.
