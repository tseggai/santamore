## INTERNAL ORGANISATION

### Article 9

The bodies of the association are the Assembly and the person authorised for representation.

## THE ASSEMBLY

### Article 10

The Assembly is the highest governing body of the association.

### Article 11

The Assembly consists of all members of the organisation.

### Article 12

Powers of the Assembly:

- adopts the Statute of the association;
- adopts amendments and supplements to the Statute;
- elects and dismisses the person authorised for representation;
- elects and dismisses the other bodies of the association;
- decides on joining federations and other forms of association of associations;
- adopts the work plan for the coming year and the report on work for the previous calendar year;
- adopts the annual financial report;
- decides on changes of goal and activities, on the termination of work and on the distribution of the remaining assets of the association;
- decides on other matters for which the Statute does not establish the competence of other bodies of the association;
- disposes of the association's assets;
- adopts other general acts of the association.

## CONVENING THE ASSEMBLY

### Article 13

The Assembly may be regular or extraordinary.

The Assembly holds a regular session once a year. The regular session of the Assembly is convened by the President of the Assembly.

The President of the Assembly is a person elected from among the members of the Assembly, by decision of the Assembly, for a term of ___________ years.

An extraordinary session of the Assembly may be scheduled on the initiative of at least one third of the membership, no later than 30 days from the day the request for its convening is submitted.

The Assembly is convened by written invitation stating the time and place of the session and the proposed agenda, at least 10 days before the session.

The relevant material (reports, analyses, balance sheets, draft decisions, etc.) is delivered together with the invitation to the Assembly.

The work of the Assembly is directed by the President of the Assembly.

## QUORUM FOR WORK AND DECISION-MAKING

### Article 14

The Assembly may sit and decide if 50 percent plus one (an absolute majority) of the members of the association are present.

The Assembly may sit and decide if it is attended by members of the association who are the authorised proxies of more than 50 percent of the total number of members.

The quorum for work and decision-making is established by the President of the Assembly, who opens its session.

### Article 15

If the Assembly could not be held for lack of a quorum, it is reconvened within seven days with the same agenda.

### Article 16

The Assembly decides on the adoption of the Statute and its amendments and supplements by a two-thirds majority of the votes of the members of the association present or represented by proxy.

The initiative to start the procedure for amending and supplementing the Statute may be raised by any member of the Assembly and by the person authorised for representation.

### Article 17

On other matters within its remit, the Assembly decides by a majority of the votes of the members of the association present.

### Article 18

The term of the Assembly is ___ years.

The Assembly may be recalled before the end of its term, on the proposal of at least half of the members of the Assembly.

All members of the organisation decide on a proposal to recall the Assembly.

The decision on recalling / ending the term of the Assembly is adopted by a two-thirds majority of all members of the Assembly.

## MANNER OF DECISION-MAKING

### Article 19

The Assembly decides by open vote. Open voting is carried out by a show of hands or by roll-call of the members.

### Article 20

The Assembly decides by secret ballot on particular matters when this is requested by at least 50 percent plus one of the members of the association.

Secret voting is carried out by ballot papers.

### Article 21

An absent member may also vote in writing.

A letter by which an absent member votes on items on the agenda is valid and is taken into account if it reaches the Assembly before the session is held.

The President of the Assembly is obliged to inform the members present how the absent member voted.

## EXCLUSION OF THE RIGHT TO VOTE

### Article 22

A member of the Assembly may not vote when decisions are taken on matters of:

- releasing that member from particular obligations and responsibilities;
- establishing claims the association has against that member;
- initiating or withdrawing from a dispute against that member;
- in other cases where the member has an interest contrary to the interests of the Association.

Whether a member's interest under paragraph 4 of this Article is contrary to the interest of the Association is assessed by the Assembly in each specific case.

The exclusion of a member's right to vote applies only to the matter that gave rise to the exclusion.

## MINUTES OF THE WORK OF THE ASSEMBLY

### Article 23

Minutes of the work of the Assembly are kept at every session of the Assembly.

The minutes record the basic data on the work of the Assembly, in particular:

- the place and day of the session
- the agenda
- the names of the members present
- the personal name of the president or of the chair (if the president cannot attend the session for justified reasons)
- the personal name of the recorder
- the course of the work, in particular the matters discussed, the names of the persons who took part in the discussion and a summary of their statements
- the result of the vote on each item of the agenda
- the chair's confirmation that decisions were adopted
- dissenting opinions of members
- the time the session closed

### Article 24

Every decision of the Assembly is entered in the minutes.

Evidence of the convening of the Assembly is attached to the minutes.

### Article 25

The minutes are signed by the President of the Assembly and the recorder.

If the minutes consist of several pages, the President and the recorder place their initials on every page of the minutes.

### Article 26

Members of the Assembly have the right to inspect the minutes once they have been drawn up and signed by the chair and the recorder.

The minutes of the work of the Assembly are kept in the archive of the Association as a document of permanent value.

## THE PERSON AUTHORISED FOR REPRESENTATION

(choose one: Executive Director, President, General Secretary)

### Article 27

The person authorised for representation is elected by decision of the Assembly of the Association for a term of ___________ years.

The decision on appointment sets the day on which the Executive Director of the association takes office.

## POWERS OF THE PERSON AUTHORISED FOR REPRESENTATION

### Article 28

The person authorised for representation:

- concludes contracts and takes other legal actions in the name and on behalf of the association;
- is responsible for the legality of the work;
- conducts the affairs of the association in accordance with the decisions of the Assembly;
- submits the draft annual financial report to the Assembly;
- performs other tasks in accordance with the law, the Statute and other acts of the Association.

## RESPONSIBILITY OF THE PERSON AUTHORISED FOR REPRESENTATION

### Article 29

The person authorised for representation is accountable for their work to the Assembly and periodically submits reports on their work to it.

For every session of the Assembly of the association, the person authorised for representation is obliged to submit a report on their work for the period between two sessions.

## DISMISSAL OF THE PERSON AUTHORISED FOR REPRESENTATION

### Article 30

The Executive Director may be dismissed from office in the following cases:

- if there are losses in the operations of the Association;
- if, through negligent or unprofessional work or by exceeding their powers, they cause damage to the Association, or if damage could have arisen as a result;
- because of inability to organise and conduct the tasks entrusted to them and the tasks within their remit.

### Article 31

The Assembly of the association decides on the dismissal of the Executive Director.

The decision of the Assembly of the association on the dismissal of the Executive Director is final.

## OFFICERS (STAFF)

### Article 32

Staff will be employed in accordance with the Law, according to the Act on the Systematisation of Positions adopted by the Assembly of the association.

## AFFILIATION

### Article 33

The Association may affiliate with and join federations or other appropriate associations in the country and abroad.

## FINANCING

### Article 34

The Association acquires assets from membership fees, voluntary contributions, gifts, donations, bequests, interest on deposits, dividends, rent, income from economic activity and in other ways not contrary to the law.

## CARRYING OUT ECONOMIC ACTIVITY

### Article 35

Within the scope of its activities, the Association carries out the following economic activities: ______________________________________________.

## MEMBERSHIP IN THE ORGANISATION

### Article 36

All legal and natural persons may be members of the Association if they accept the goals and activities defined by the Statute of the Association, on the recommendation of one of the members.

The decision on admission to membership is taken by the Assembly of the association by majority vote.

Membership is acquired by signing a membership application form.

A register of members is kept as the record of the association's members.

## TERMINATION OF MEMBERSHIP

### Article 37

A member may withdraw from membership by giving a written statement of withdrawal.

Membership in the Association also ends on the basis of a decision of the Assembly in the event of non-compliance with the provisions of this Statute or damage to the reputation of the association.

Any member of the Association may submit an initiative for expulsion from the Association.

A member must be given the opportunity to respond to the reasons for which the proposal to adopt a decision terminating their membership in the Association was submitted.

## RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF MEMBERS

### Article 38

A member has the right to:

- participate, on an equal footing with other members, in achieving the goals of the Association;
- participate directly in decision-making at the Assembly, and through the bodies of the Association;
- elect and be elected to the bodies of the Association;
- be informed in a timely and complete manner about the work and activities of the Association.

A member is obliged to:

- actively contribute to achieving the goals of the Association;
- participate, according to their interests, in the activities of the Association;
- pay the membership fee;
- perform other tasks entrusted to them by the Assembly of the association.

## TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 39

An initiative to start the procedure for the termination of the association's work may be raised by one third of the members of the Assembly.

The Assembly decides on the termination of the association's work by a two-thirds majority of the votes of the members of the association present.

### Article 40

In the event of termination of work, the organisation's assets will be allocated to another non-governmental organisation or public institution that carries out the same or a similar activity.

The decision on the disposal of assets is adopted by the Assembly by a two-thirds majority of all members.

### Article 41

The provisions of the Law on Non-Governmental Organisations apply directly to all matters not regulated by this Statute.

### Article 42

This Statute enters into force on the day of registration.
