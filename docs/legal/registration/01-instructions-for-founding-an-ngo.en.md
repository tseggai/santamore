# Instructions for founding an NGO

English translation of *Instrukcije za osnivanje NVO* (Montenegrin original supplied to the team, September 2026). Translation only; nothing added or removed. Editorial notes are marked **[Translator's note]**.

## 1. Registration with the Ministry

You are registered on the basis of an application for entry. Together with the application for entry you submit the founding decision, the minutes of the founding assembly and the statute, as well as photocopies of the founders' identity cards. An association must have a minimum of three founders, whereas a foundation can be founded by a single person. A seal must also be made.

The statute, the founding decision, the minutes of the founding assembly and the application for entry are submitted to the registry office (archive) of the Ministry of Regional-Investment Development and Cooperation with Non-Governmental Organisations (Eco-efficient building, Cetinjski put bb, Podgorica). You receive the number under which your case has been filed. The head of the Directorate for the Registration of Non-Governmental Organisations and Political Parties at the Ministry of Regional-Investment Development and Cooperation with Non-Governmental Organisations is Milan Šestović (tel. 068/878-096, email: milan.sestovic@mirn.gov.me). After receiving the decision on registration, the seal must be made.

## 2. Classification of activity at MONSTAT

At the Statistical Office (MONSTAT), obtain the form for the classification of the activity the NGO engages in, complete the form and deliver it to MONSTAT together with a copy of the decision on registration from the Ministry of Regional-Investment Development and Cooperation with Non-Governmental Organisations. This procedure can also be completed electronically by following the instructions at the LINK. **[Translator's note: the link was not preserved in the copy we received.]**

If you are not registering for economic activity, go to step 4.

## 3. Economic activity: Central Register of Business Entities (CRPS)

If the NGO is registering to carry out an economic activity, the following must be submitted to the Central Register of Business Entities (CRPS):

- a certified decision on registration issued by the MJU **[Translator's note: MJU = Ministry of Public Administration, the ministry's former name; it is the same registration decision as in step 1]**
- a certified notice of classification from MONSTAT
- a decision of the competent NGO body on entry of the economic activity in the CRPS (a decision of your organisation's Assembly that the economic activity be entered in the CRPS and that the person authorised for representation (president, director) is charged with registering that fact), with the signature of the recorder and the signature of the chair of the Assembly
- the statute of the non-governmental organisation, signed by all founders
- a certified founding decision
- a certified decision on the appointment of the authorised person
- the minutes of the Assembly session
- court fee: 10 EUR
- certified copies of identity cards or passports (if the founders are foreigners)
- the application forms, which can be found on the CRPS website

After the NGO has been registered in the CRPS, it is necessary to obtain an approval (decision) for operation from the Secretariat for Entrepreneurship Development, which establishes that the conditions for the requested activity / type of business premises are met (if the NGO wishes to engage in economic activity).

## 4. Opening a bank account

- a copy of the decision on registration from the MJU
- a copy of the notice of classification by activity, from MONSTAT
- OP form / the signature of the authorised person certified at the court (fee of 5 EUR). The authorised person must go to the court in person. (The OP form is obtained at the bank and certified at the Basic Court.)
- a request to open an account (form obtained at the bank)
- a specimen signature card (form obtained at the bank)
- a contract on opening and maintaining the account (form obtained at the bank)
- proof of payment (form obtained at the bank)
- if the NGO is registered in the CRPS, a confirmation of entry in the CRPS must also be provided
- PIB (tax identification number)
- a copy of the identity card or passport (if a foreigner)

## 5. Tax identification number (PIB)

An NGO must have a PIB. When going to the Tax Administration, bring:

- a certified decision on entry in the register of NGOs issued by the Ministry
- an extract from the classification register from the Statistical Office (MONSTAT)
- the application form, which is obtained at the Tax Administration
- the founding decision
- photocopies of the founders' identity cards
- the statute

All information about the PIB can be obtained from the Tax Administration on 19707.

**[Translator's note: step 4 (bank) lists the PIB as a requirement, and the PIB is obtained in step 5. The working order is therefore 1 → 2 → (3, only if registering an economic activity) → 5 → 4.]**
