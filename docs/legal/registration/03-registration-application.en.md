# Application for entry in the Register of Non-Governmental Organisations (template)

English translation of *Obrazac prijave za upis u registar MIRN za udruženje*. Blanks are reproduced as in the original.

Government of Montenegro
Ministry of Regional-Investment Development and Cooperation with Non-Governmental Organisations

**Subject:** Application for entry in the Register of Non-Governmental Organisations

**Applicant:** _____________________

## Explanation

On ___________________, the Founding Assembly of _____________________________________ met and adopted the following decisions:

1. to found an association named: _____________
2. to adopt the Statute
3. to submit an application for entry in the Register of Non-Governmental Organisations.

In accordance with the decisions listed above, we ask you to enter the non-governmental organisation "________________________________________" in the Register of Non-Governmental Organisations kept at your Ministry.

Enclosed we submit:

- the Decision on founding
- the Minutes of the founding assembly
- the Statute

In ________________, on _____________.

Chair of the Founding Assembly, s.r.

**[Translator's note: in the original, items 1 and 2 of the decisions list are unnumbered and only item 3 carries a number. The heading block "Government of Montenegro / Ministry ..." is the addressee.]**
