# How to complete the registration pack / Kako dovršiti paket za registraciju

This is the working guide for the founders. It says what to collect, what to decide, which form each fact lands in, and what each draft is for. Open the forms and drafts at `/admin/registracija` in the console (staff only). Montenegrin is the operative text of everything that is filed; the English side is a translation for founders who do not read it. Nothing in this pack is legal advice: the lawyer reviews the Montenegrin before anything is signed.

## 1. What to do, in order

1. **Collect the facts** in section 2. Every square-bracketed value in the forms is one of them.
2. **Fill the blue blanks** in the console, in the language you think in. One value serves every form: the founders' names appear in the Decision, the Statute and the Minutes at once.
3. **Switch language** once you are done. The blanks you changed are translated; read the other side, correct it, then press Save.
4. **Take the decisions** in section 3 (statute option, terms, seal, Executive Director).
5. **Send the lawyer** the Montenegrin side of the four filing documents (02, 03, 04, 05) and the Statute additions draft. Ask the questions in section 6 of the additions.
6. **Hold the founding assembly**: all founders present, agenda as in the Minutes, adopt the Decision and the Statute, elect the President and Deputy of the Assembly and the Executive Director. Note the start and end time; those go into the Minutes.
7. **Print** each filing document from the console (Print / PDF, Montenegrin side), sign where the lines are, and file them at the Ministry with photocopies of every founder's identity card. Tick the steps in form 01 as you go.
8. **After the registration decision arrives**: order the seal, register the activity code at MONSTAT, open the bank account, get the PIB. Form 01 lists each step with the office it belongs to.
9. **Then the drafts**: the Board adopts the policies in section 4 at its first session, and the public ones go on the website before the first event or donation.

## 2. Facts to collect (the blanks)

| Fact | Blank in the console | Used in | Who has it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founders' full names, JMB (personal number), home addresses; three founders are the legal minimum, five blanks are available | Founder 1 to 5 | 02 Decision, 04 Statute (signatures), 05 Minutes | Each founder, from the identity card |
| Address of the seat (street and number, Tivat) | Address | 02, 03, 05 | Whoever provides the premises; the Ministry needs a real address, a founder's home is acceptable |
| Date of the founding assembly | Date of the founding assembly | 02, 03, 04, 05 | Set by the founders |
| Start and end time, venue of the assembly | Start time, End time, Venue address | 05 | Set by the founders |
| Who chairs the assembly, who takes the minutes, who is Deputy President | Chair, Recorder, Deputy | 04, 05 | Elected at the assembly; decide beforehand |
| The Executive Director (the person authorised for representation): name, JMB, address | Authorised person | 02, 03, 05 | The founders' decision; this person signs contracts and the Monri and bank paperwork |
| Chair's phone and email for the Ministry | Applicant | 03 | The chair |
| Filing date | Filing date | 03 | The day you hand the documents in |
| Description of the mark in the centre of the seal | Symbol in the centre of the seal | 04 Art. 8 | Brand: the Santamore icon; describe it in words |

Goals, activities, economic activities, the seal's rim text and the four-year terms are already filled from the brief and the team guide. Check them; change them only with the lawyer.

## 3. Decisions to take before the assembly

- **Statute option.** The template statute (form 04) is the minimum the Ministry accepts. The draft "Statute additions" adds the Board, the Grants Committee, chapters and proxy voting. Recommended: adopt the additions at founding, because a later change means a new assembly and a new filing. If adopted, the lawyer merges them into form 04 and renumbers the articles.
- **Terms.** Four years for the President of the Assembly, the Assembly and the Executive Director are pre-filled (the template's default). Shorter is allowed.
- **Economic activity.** Article 35 lists the economic activities (event fees, merchandise). Keep it only if you intend to register with the Central Register of Business Entities (step 3 in form 01); otherwise the accountant advises removing it.
- **Short name.** "NVU „Santamore“" is pre-filled; the Ministry accepts any short form that is not misleading.

## 4. The drafts: what each is, what it hangs on, what to add

Every draft is bilingual: Montenegrin paragraph, then its English translation. Edit either side in the console; the drafts do not auto-translate, so change both. Items in `[[PLACEHOLDER: …]]` are unknown facts or Board decisions. "Reviews" says who must read it before adoption.

| Draft | What it is | Linked to | What to add | Reviews |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statute additions | Five new chapters for the Statute: Board (Arts. 32 to 35), Grants Committee (36 to 39), chapters (40 to 44), proxy voting (17a to 17f), plus the edits they force in existing articles | Form 04; adopted at the founding assembly if chosen in section 3 | Names of the first Board members if you want them in the Minutes; answers to "Points for the lawyer" at the end | Lawyer |
| Grants Committee criteria | Who can receive funds, how much, in what procedure, what is published | Statute additions Art. 38; the beneficiary application form; the public ledger | The Board's numbers: standard ceiling per decision, unanimity threshold, urgent-aid ceiling, cooling-off period (section 12 suggests values) | Grants Committee proposes, Board adopts, accountant reads |
| Chapter Rules | How a chapter (Kotor, Podgorica…) is founded, run, funded and closed | Statute additions Arts. 40 to 44 | Minimum members to found a chapter, the share of chapter income kept locally (team guide says 70 %), the chapter lead's spending limit | Board |
| Conflict of interest policy | Who declares what, who steps out of which vote, the register of interests | Statute additions Arts. 34 and 39; Grants Committee criteria | Nothing factual; decide the annual declaration date | Board, lawyer for the Montenegrin |
| Child safeguarding policy | Rules for events with children, designated safeguarding lead, reporting | Event terms; volunteer agreement; Code of conduct | Name and contact of the safeguarding lead; minimum ages per distance and activity | Board, lawyer (police-check rules under Montenegrin law) |
| Event terms and waiver | What a participant accepts when registering: risk, medical fitness, photos, refunds | The event registration flow on the site; the Statute's goals | Refund cut-off in days, minimum age table, the competent court for Tivat | Lawyer; insurer if you take event insurance |
| Donation policy | What we accept, what we refuse, restricted gifts, refunds, anonymity, public ledger | Grants Committee criteria; the public ledger; Monri terms | Bank, IBAN once the account exists; the refund window; the amount above which a donor is named in the annual report | Board, accountant |
| Privacy policy | GDPR-style notice: what data, why, how long, processors (Supabase, Vercel, Monri, Strava) | Terms of use; impressum; the site's cookie and registration flows | Contact email; hosting regions; the processor's full legal name from the Monri contract | Lawyer |
| Impressum | The organisation details every public page must carry | Registration decision; PIB; bank account | Registration number, PIB, VAT status, IBAN, address, contact email, activity code from MONSTAT (every one is a placeholder until the offices issue them) | Accountant |
| Terms of use | Rules for using the website: accounts, fundraiser pages, content, liability | Privacy policy; event terms; donation policy | Contact email, competent court, the domain | Lawyer |
| Code of conduct | Behaviour expected of members, volunteers, staff and Board; sanctions | Statute Arts. 12 and 18 (membership, expulsion); the safeguarding policy | Who receives complaints (name or role) | Board |
| Volunteer agreement | The short sign-up declaration for event volunteers and the full agreement for regular volunteers | Code of conduct; safeguarding; the Law on Volunteer Work | Minimum volunteer age, insurance decision, agreement numbering, contact | Lawyer (Law on Volunteer Work) |
| Sponsorship agreement | Contract template with a sponsor and the rules on which sponsors we take | Donation policy; the sponsors table on the site | Contract number, amounts and in-kind values per sponsor, VAT status, bank details, the excluded industries list | Lawyer, accountant |
| Beneficiary application | The form a person or organisation fills in to ask for help, and how the Committee handles it | Grants Committee criteria; the beneficiaries pages | Contact email and postal address for applications; the documents you require as proof | Grants Committee |

## 5. Before you file: a last check

- [ ] Goals (Art. 6) and activities (Art. 7) are identical in the Decision and the Statute (they are the same blanks; check nothing was edited on one side only).
- [ ] Every founder in the Decision appears in the Minutes and signs the Statute.
- [ ] The Executive Director named in the Minutes is the person in the Application and Article 7 of the Decision.
- [ ] Dates match: assembly date in 02, 03, 04 and 05; filing date in 03 only.
- [ ] No square brackets remain on the Montenegrin side of 02, 03, 04, 05 (search the printed page for "[").
- [ ] The lawyer has returned the Montenegrin side with comments applied.
- [ ] Printed in Montenegrin, signed in ink where the lines are, identity-card copies attached, one copy kept.
