Why it's built this way

Amount before identity

The commitment is made in the first three seconds, before a single form field appears. Middle amount pre-selected. Every chip carries an impact line, because 25 € means nothing and "a week of school meals" means something.

The fee toggle is the whole business model

Pre-checked, with live arithmetic. This is what lets you keep saying 100% of donations reach the cause while paying a team — the donor covers the card fee, sponsors cover the salaries.

Two payment rails, not one

Stripe doesn't serve Montenegrin merchants and PayPal can't receive here, so cards go through a local acquirer. Tap Bankovni prenos: Montenegro joined SEPA in October 2025, so a unique reference per fundraiser plus an EPC QR code gives you a near-zero-fee rail that auto-credits the right page.

Monthly framed as arithmetic

"€10 a month is €120 a year" converts better than a bare toggle. Recurring revenue is what makes an employment contract signable.

The waterline

Santa of the water, made literal. Progress isn't a bar — the page floods. Tap Doniraj 25 € and watch the level rise and the donor land on the wall. This is the one flourish in the design; everything else stays quiet.

The story is the product

Personalised pages raise several times more than default ones, so onboarding should refuse to publish a page without a photo, a target and three sentences in the runner's own voice.

The ask list

Fundraisers who send 15+ messages raise about 76% more. So the app doesn't just host a page, it hands over a named list, a written message and a Viber button. Viber, not just WhatsApp — this is the Balkans.

Donor messages, deliberately public

They're the persuasion engine. Each one is social proof, and each one gives the runner something to share that isn't another ask.

Teams beat individuals

A company sponsorship that includes a team slot turns 18 employees into fundraisers instead of one logo on a banner. Schools are the same trick with a trophy instead of a budget.

Watch activation, not sign-ups

412 registered · 118 fundraising is the honest pair of numbers. The sector-wide trend is more participants raising less each — chase activated fundraisers, not registration counts.

Cash gets logged too

In a cash economy, a runner who collects €120 at the office must be able to record it. Otherwise the leaderboard lies, and a leaderboard that lies stops motivating anyone.

This page is your moat

Money in and money out on one screen, no login, generated from the database rather than typed into a PDF. Nearly nobody in the region does this. It answers "where does the money go?" before anyone asks, and it converts sceptics into sponsors.

The two funds, side by side

Donations to beneficiaries. Sponsorship, entry fees and grants to salaries. Publishing the split is what makes a paid team compatible with the promise you made last year.

Documentation chips

Every disbursement carries proof — receipt, invoice, signed handover, board decision. Beneficiary names stay private by default; the evidence stays available to an auditor.

Append-only

Nothing is editable once published. Corrections are new, dated, visible rows. A ledger that can be quietly rewritten isn't a ledger.

Prototype only — placeholder names, amounts and beneficiaries. No live payment connection.
